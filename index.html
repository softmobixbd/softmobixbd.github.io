NumIQ (Math Puzzle & IQ Game) New

Sharpen your mind, one puzzle a day.

Puzzle 100+ downloads Rated 3+ 1v1 Battle Mode

A math and logic puzzle game built for a real mental workout — not ads disguised as a game. Solve number sequences, equations, geometric patterns and addition pyramids, take a fresh Daily Challenge tuned to your level, and go head-to-head in live 1v1 Battle Mode against another player (or a skill-matched bot when no one's online). Color Flood (Flood It Puzzle) New

Every board is winnable. Prove it in par.

Strategy 10+ downloads Rated 3+ v2.0.1

A flood-fill strategy puzzle: pick a color, and every matching tile touching your corner joins you — turn the whole board one color before the moves run out. Every board is solver-checked to be winnable before you see it, and a beam-search solver sets a par to earn your stars against. Three ways to play — Journey, Daily Challenge and Zen — across four tiers from a relaxed 10×10 to a ruthless 22×22. 12 contrast-validated palettes, a colorblind glyph mode, no banner ads, and no account or internet needed.

NumXross

Where numbers meet the cross.

A crossword built on numbers instead of letters. Place number tiles so every row and every column satisfies its equation, using standard order of operations. Every puzzle is verified to have exactly one solution before it's ever served, and lazy ×1 or +0 filler is filtered out at generation time.

  • Easy, Medium, Hard & Expert grids
  • Shared Daily Challenge — four puzzles a day, global leaderboard
  • Streaks, achievements & a personal NumXross IQ score
  • Zero forced interstitials, unlimited free undo, 3 free hints per puzzle
  • Offline play with light, dark & AMOLED themes

About Us

Small studio, purposeful games.

SoftMobiXBD is a mobile app and game development studio based in Bangladesh, founded and led by Farjana Akter. We build interactive, meaningful digital experiences — games that are useful, enjoyable, and quietly educational.

With mobile screen time only growing, we set out to make it count. Today four of our games are live on Google Play — Quick Math Quest, Color Rush, NumIQ and Color Flood — with NumXross next in line. Each one is free to download, playable offline, free of intrusive monetization, and genuinely fun enough to open every day — whether that means sharpening algebra skills, training reflexes, or exercising logic.

Every app we ship comes with a dedicated privacy policy and terms of service — reviewed and published before it ever reaches Google Play.
